Capabilities

Built for real conversations

Everything you expect from a modern chat model, in an interface that gets out of the way.

Natural conversation

Talk the way you'd talk to a person. C10.1 follows the thread, remembers what you've said, and asks when something's unclear.

Code that works

Snippets in Python, JavaScript, CSS and more - syntax highlighted, copyable in one click, with an explanation of why it's written that way.

Explains properly

Science, technology, how things work. Answers built to leave you understanding the thing, not just holding a definition.

Instant responses

No queue, no cold start, no spinner that outlasts your patience. Answers begin streaming the moment you hit send.

Private by default

Your conversations are stored in your own browser and nowhere else. Nothing is uploaded, and there's no account to create.

Scales up with you

Point C10.1 at a larger model with your own API key and the whole interface carries over - same chats, same shortcuts, more depth.

What it does well, and what it doesn't

A first version should be honest about its edges. Here are both sides.

Where it's strong

  • Explaining science and technology topics in plain language
  • Common coding tasks, with working snippets and reasoning
  • Thinking through decisions and breaking down problems
  • Arithmetic, short writing, brainstorming and first drafts
  • Speed - answers start immediately, with nothing to install

Where it falls short

  • No internet access, so nothing about current events or live data
  • No memory between separate conversations
  • Narrower knowledge than a large hosted model - it will say so rather than bluff
  • Long multi-step reasoning is not its strength yet
  • Connect your own API key when you need more depth than v1 can give
