Update Log

V1.63 beta
  • Six more songs, twelve in all — including Big Wave
  • The music player UI is gone. All that is left is the track title at the top of the screen, with notes drifting up out of it
  • The playlist carries straight through into a match now instead of stopping at the lobby door — same song, no break
  • M skips a track on a keyboard
  • Fixed the title sitting on top of the wave banner; the HUD, title, banner, boss bar and squad list are one clean stack now
V1.62
  • The lobby plays a six-track playlist from a music folder next to the game — straight through, then round again
  • Now-playing strip in the lobby with the track name, elapsed time, a progress bar and skip buttons
  • Leaving for a match pauses the playlist and hands over to the map score; coming back picks the same song up where it left off
  • A track that will not play is stepped over rather than taking the whole playlist down; if none of them play the built-in score comes back
  • Music files not there? The game says so once and carries on with the built-in score
V1.61
  • Background music — four original pieces played live on the game's own synth: a warm, unhurried one for the lobby, and a track each for the Grassy Field, The Forest and The Volcano. Nothing to download, so it starts instantly
  • The score leans on its low end while a boss is on the field
  • Settings: separate Music slider and an on/off switch. Volume, music level and the switch are all saved between sessions
  • Music pauses when you tab away
  • Want your own music instead? There is a CUSTOM_MUSIC block near the top of the file — put an audio filename or URL against a screen and the game loops that instead of the built-in track
V1.6
  • Fixed the walk: every character in the game bent their knees the wrong way, snapping the shin forward through the joint. Players, teammates, lobby crowds and all eleven zombie types now walk, plod and sprint properly, with the heel kicking up behind on the swing
  • The spawn is a portal now, not a pile of grey spheres — stepped dais, carved arch and keystone, counter-rotating rune rings, embers pouring out of the mouth, braziers and rune stones, themed per map
  • New map: The Volcano — 25 waves, ash falling, lava pools, burnt spars and a smoking cone. Unlocked by clearing The Forest
  • Five new enemies: Ash Walker, Ember Runner, Magma Brute, Cinder Priest, and the boss — The Corpse, 3,000 HP
  • New tower: The Slasher — furnace-heated twin blades, 10 dmg every 0.40s at Lv1 up to 25 every 0.30s at Lv4, range 3 → 4, $600. Shortest reach in the game
  • Press Play in the lobby to matchmake — the queue fills your squad and hands you straight to the map vote
  • Coming soon: The Blocky Event, listed on the lobby Event board
  • Teammates fixed: they bank cash toward the next level instead of dribbling it on more level-1 towers, they have a per-wave tower budget, they place to cover track nothing else covers and favour corners, and they stroll between spots and stand watching their own towers rather than freezing on the spot
  • The map vote is rebuilt for three maps and shows each map's difficulty and coin payout
V1.52
  • Every tower re-animated: they now stand easy with the weapon down when nothing is in range and shoulder it when a zombie walks in, recoil rolls through the shoulder into the torso and head at a weight that suits the weapon, and the body leads and banks into a turn instead of spinning like a turntable
  • Idle behaviour: the Scout checks his binoculars, the Cop and Survivor stretch, the Commander directs the line with his stick, and they all sweep the field between waves
  • New tower: The Commander — bought once with coins, then deployed for $1,200. Sidearm plus an aura: every friendly tower in his ring gets +15% damage and +10% fire rate at Lv1, up to +40% and +28% at Lv4. Auras do not stack
  • Coins: clear a map for its full payout, lose and you still bank half. Grassy Field pays 500, The Forest pays 700. Coins and unlocks are saved between sessions
  • The Cop rebalanced — he was the worst value in the game. Baton now 9 dmg on a 1.0s swing up to 40, and the taser is a weaker but stunning fallback, so the baton breaking is a real loss
  • Zombies now show their remaining HP as a number above the bar
  • Patient Zero cut to 1,200 HP; new boss The Grave Digger, 2,500 HP, hood and shovel, closes out The Forest
  • Picking your squad size at the desk drops you straight into the map vote
  • The boss bar now names whichever boss is on the field and reads out exact HP

  • The lobby is a place now: walk to the Loadout Shop, the Squad Desk, the Notice Board or the Deploy Pad

  • Fixed the joystick pushing you the wrong way sideways — right now means right

  • Teammates now spawn beside you off the track, and stand down once a run is decided
