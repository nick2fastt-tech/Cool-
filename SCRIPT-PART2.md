# BATTLE WARS — PART TWO

95 scenes. Genre: Action · Sci-Fi · Mystery · Drama. Timeline: 1946 → present day.

The core question: who killed the original Blue Team member, why were the team's
memories erased, and how does that connect to the gold beneath the Earth.

## Cast

**Blue Team** — Kai, Jace, Maya, Dre, Leo
**Red Team** — Ryder, Zane, Nova, Blaze, Ace
**1946** — Elias, Marcus, and the Blue Team of their day
**Victor** — the man in the dark coat, in both eras
**The Collector** — the same man, masked, eighty years later

## Structure

| Act | Scenes | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I — The end before the beginning | 1–5 | The ruined city, in rain |
| II — 1946 | 6–51 | Headquarters, the alley, the archive, the road, the facility |
| III — Present day | 52–70 | The command centre, the tunnel, the chamber |
| IV — The Collector | 71–90 | The gold chamber |
| V — The destruction | 91–95 | The tunnel, and back to the ruins of scene 1 |

The opening scene is the ending. Act I shows the wreckage without explanation;
Act V arrives at the same rainy street from the other side, and by then the
audience knows what caused it.
