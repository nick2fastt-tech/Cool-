T10 WORLD

A city that keeps going whether you do anything or not.
No story. No objectives. You wake up on the street.
Everything else, you ask T10 for.

The name shapes the city — different names, different streets.

PC: WASD to move · mouse to look (or drag) · E for everything · F vehicles · 1–0 and Z X C J B N for powers · T for T10 · M map · Esc settings
Mobile: left thumb to walk · right thumb to look · buttons bottom-right · powers top-left
