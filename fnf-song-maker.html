    Optimization

    Five things the game can stop doing when a device is struggling. Each is off unless you turn it on — the game already sheds resolution by itself when it misses frames.

    Optimization
    Master switch for all five
    1 · Performance Mode
    Render at a lower resolution from the start
    2 · Low Effects
    No note splashes, popups or glows
    3 · Sprite Optimization
    Fewer character redraws, coarser pose blending
    4 · Note Optimization
    Draw notes flat, and only just before they arrive
    5 · Background Optimization
    Freeze the stage and skip its animation
