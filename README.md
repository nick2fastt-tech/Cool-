## Games
- **ZShooter.html**: Zombie Shooter Alpha V1.01. Open it in a browser and play.
  - **World 1: The White Room**, waves 1–10, starting with an 18-second elevator cutscene.
  - XP and levels (30–45 XP per kill, 100 XP per level). Progress saves automatically.
  - Weapons: ZS-17 Pistol and the ZR-4 Rifle, which unlocks at Level 10.
  - Dying gives a 30-second respawn: spectate your squad while your body rises as a zombie.
  - **Legacy Mode** (in Settings): the original Castle Mode, waves 21–35.
- **horror.html**: The Last Candle.
