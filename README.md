## Games
- **ZShooter.html**: Zombie Shooter Alpha V1.05. Open it in a browser and play.
  - **World 1: The White Room**, waves 1–10, starting with an 18-second elevator cutscene.
  - **World 2: The Corrupted Room**, waves 11–20. Clearing World 1 plays a cutscene that takes the squad down to World 2.
    - World 2 is a dark version of the white room: slimy black concrete and flickering purple lights.
    - Zombies come through a wall portal fed by purple beams. A sealed portal in the floor opens for the bosses.
    - **New enemies:**
      - Corrupted Zombie and Corrupted Runner
      - **Dog Zombie**: fast, and leaps at you
      - **Slimy Tall Zombie**: leaves slime puddles that slow you down
      - **The Summoner**: bullets can't hurt him. Shoot him and he kneels to summon zombies from the ground. Kill every summoned zombie before it climbs out and he is banished.
    - **Bosses:**
      - Wave 17 mini boss, **Slimy Observer**: spits slime that turns into crawlers.
      - Wave 20 boss, **Slime Observer**: adds an eye beam, a slime shockwave, and an enraged phase.
    - Some teammates carry rifles in World 2.
    - Once World 1 is cleared, World 2 can also be started from the Worlds menu.
  - **Bullet types** (Weapons menu):
    - Standard
    - Electric: yellow shock rounds, +60% damage, stun and slow enemies
    - Laser: red beam, double damage
  - Base damage: Pistol 5, Rifle 3. Headshots do double.
  - XP and levels (30–45 XP per kill, 100 XP per level). Progress saves automatically.
  - Weapons: ZS-17 Pistol and the ZR-4 Rifle, which unlocks at Level 10.
  - Dying gives a 30-second respawn: spectate your squad while your body rises as a zombie.
  - **Legacy Mode** (in Settings): the original Castle Mode, waves 21–35.
- **horror.html**: The Last Candle.
