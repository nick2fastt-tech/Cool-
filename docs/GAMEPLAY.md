# How to play

## Controls (touch)

| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Move | Left half of screen — a floating joystick appears under your thumb |
| Look / camera | Drag anywhere on the right half |
| Zoom | Pinch with two fingers (mouse wheel on desktop) |
| Jump | ⭡ button (bottom-right) |
| Sprint | » button (hold) |
| Game action | Contextual buttons (e.g. 🔫 Fire, 🧱 Wall, 🔥 Nitro) |
| Menu | ☰ (top-right) opens Home; 🔔 notifications; 👥 player list |
| Chat | 💬 Chat (bottom-left) |

Desktop testing: **WASD/arrows** move, **Shift** sprints, **Space** jumps, drag
to look, wheel to zoom. Controls are customizable in **Settings** (button size,
left-handed layout, vibration).

## The lobby

You spawn on the central dais. Walk into a glowing **portal** to enter a game,
or into the **Shop** / **Leaderboard** kiosks. Trees, water, mountains, clouds,
and a live day/night cycle surround the hub.

## Obby 🧗 (fully playable)

Parkour a procedurally generated course to the gold finish pad.
- **Checkpoints** (teal flags) save your respawn and pay coins.
- **Lava** and **falling off** send you back to your last checkpoint.
- **Moving platforms** oscillate; **trampolines** launch you; **blue pads** give
  a speed boost.
- Difficulty ramps up — gaps widen and platforms shrink. Finishing awards a big
  XP + coin bonus and the *Parkour Pro* achievement.
- Multiplayer: everyone in the room runs the same seeded course — race them.

## Survival 🏕️ (playable core)

Survive on a large map across the day/night cycle.
- Walk into **trees/rocks** to gather **wood/stone** (they regrow).
- **🧱 Wall** spends wood to build cover; **🍖 Eat** restores hunger.
- Hunger drains over time and, when empty, drains health.
- At **night**, enemies spawn and chase you — **⚔️ Attack** them or wall them
  out. Survive to dawn for a reward.

## Racing 🏎️ (playable core)

Arcade racing on looping tracks.
- Pass the ordered gates to complete a **lap** (anti-shortcut enforced).
- **🔥 Nitro** burns a regenerating boost meter for extra speed.
- Your **best lap** per track is saved. Multiple tracks and vehicles are
  selectable.

## Battle Arena ⚔️ (playable core)

Timed combat, **Free-For-All** or **Team**.
- **🔫 Fire** your weapon (Blaster / Scatter / Railer — different damage, range,
  cooldown) at opponents; hits spawn particle effects, kills spawn a burst.
- You and opponents **respawn** on death. A live **scoreboard** tracks kills;
  the match ends on the **timer** and rewards the winner.

## Progression

- Earn **coins** and **XP** from every activity; XP levels you up.
- **Daily rewards** escalate over a 7-day streak (claim on the Home screen).
- **Achievements** unlock automatically and pay coins.
- Spend coins in the **Shop** on faces, hair, shirts, pants, hats, and back
  accessories; equip them in **Inventory**. Everything saves locally and syncs
  to the cloud provider.
