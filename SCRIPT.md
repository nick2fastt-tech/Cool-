# RUN FROM ZOMBIES

Short film. Seven girls, 14–15 years old. One sunny afternoon.

`run-from-zombies.html` is this script, built as a real-time 3D film. The shot
list in that file follows these parts in order; the timings below are where each
part falls in the finished 5:20 cut.

---

## Part 1 — The Bedroom
Seven girls hang out in a bedroom on a bright, sunny day. They sit on the bed,
talking and laughing. They decide the weather is too good to stay inside and
agree to go to the park and play tag. They get up, grab their things, put on
their shoes, and leave.

## Part 2 — Walking to the Park
The seven walk through the sunny neighborhood. Everything feels normal.
Then one girl starts coughing. The others ask if she's okay.
> "Yeah, I'm alright."

They keep walking.

## Part 3 — Something Is Wrong
At the park she looks worse — sweating heavily, struggling to walk.
Brief first-person shot from her POV: unstable, dizzy vision.
She nearly trips; another girl catches her. They ask again.
> "I'm alright."

The others accept it.

## Part 4 — Playing Tag
The girls play tag. For a moment it's fun and normal — laughing, running,
chasing. Then the sick girl stops and collapses. The others rush over.
She's coughing violently and doesn't get up. One girl cautiously approaches.

## Part 5 — The First Attack
The girl on the ground moves. Everyone freezes. She lunges at the closest girl.
Rapid cuts between terrified faces and the struggle — the attack plays through
quick cuts and sound rather than graphic imagery. The others scream and scatter.

## Part 6 — Under the Slide
The girls run across the playground. The infected girl is distracted by the
chaos. Two girls reach the friend who was attacked and pull her under the slide
to hide her. One whispers for the others to run. The group separates and hides.

## Part 7 — "We Need a Cure"
Two girls hide together. One insists they need to find a cure or an antidote.
> "No. There is no antidote. There's no cure. Are you insane?"

Then they realize the infected girl has noticed them. She appears nearby.

## Part 8 — Another Attack
The infected girl grabs the one who wanted the cure. Quick camera move,
screaming, hard cut away — no graphic detail. The other girl escapes and runs.
Brief cut back to the infected girl: the infection is spreading.

## Part 9 — The Bathroom
The survivors regroup by the park bathroom, frightened, trying to think.
One girl remembers she brought a water gun — she'd thought they could mess
around with it. Someone points out there's only one. Then, half joking:
> "Maybe zombies hate water."

They start to wonder whether water could reverse the infection.

## Part 10 — The Water Gun Plan
They fill the water gun. They prepare themselves. They have no idea if it works.
They leave the bathroom and step back outside. The park is completely changed —
the sunny, playful mood is gone. Quiet. Frightening.

## Part 11 — The Zombies
They meet the infected girls. They raise the water gun. They spray.
Everyone waits. Nothing. The water doesn't cure them. The zombies keep coming.
The plan has failed.

## Part 12 — The Final Attack
Under the slide, the girl left there moves. She's infected now. She grabs the
girl who was helping her. The girl struggles free and runs toward the bathroom
group, screaming:
> "RUN!"

The others turn. The infection has spread again.

## Part 13 — The Water Gun Falls
The girl with the water gun tries again. The zombie attacks. The water gun hits
the ground. The girls scream and run. The camera holds on the abandoned water
gun while footsteps and chaos continue off-screen.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

## Part 14 — The Ending
Silence. The park slowly reappears. Nobody is running. The seven girls stand
together — all of them zombies now. They stare into the camera.
A final unsettling sound.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

## Title Card
**RUN FROM ZOMBIES**

Credits.
